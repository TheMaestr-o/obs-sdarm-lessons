⚠️ Browser ≠ OBS What you see here in a regular browser (Chrome/Safari) isn't a guarantee of the same look inside OBS. OBS renders a Browser Source through its own embedded engine (CEF), which may differ in font versions and spacing. Opening files here is fine for a quick check of text and logic, but always verify the final look by adding the file as a real Browser Source inside OBS itself — that's the only way to see what viewers will actually see.

Lesson question overlay fully offline in use

panel.htmlcontrol panel — question text, template, colors result.htmlthe overlay itself — this is what you add to OBS as a Browser Source
  1. Open both links in two tabs of the same browser
  2. In the panel, click a section number and then a question letter (or type line 1 and line 2 yourself), pick a style, and press Show
  3. The overlay plays its entrance once and then holds indefinitely — it does not auto-hide. Pick the next question and press Show again: the plate stays, only the line that changed moves. Stop takes it down, and is lit red for as long as something is up
Look: Slash & Slide is the author's own design — a translucent plate cut at 13°, a gold stripe riding the cut, the heading in gold capitals, the question in white with its scripture reference in gold italic; the plate grows when a question wraps. Slide Up / Down is centred over a scrim. The easing both share comes from CodePen mattchestnut/dMrONe, the reference we were pointed at in the first place. Background is true CSS transparency — no Chroma Key filter needed in OBS. A third style, "Quiet Rule", still renders for id=3 but the panel offers these two (see LOCAL-SETUP.md). All 22 lesson languages are drawn in fonts that ship with the project, Japanese, Chinese and Thai included. See AUTHORSHIP.md for exactly what is ours and what is borrowed.
Each preview runs the entrance once and then keeps going for another two seconds — that flat, unchanging tail is the point. On stream it stays there until the presenter moves on. The dark background stands in for the video underneath; the overlay itself is transparent.
